03 / AUDIO & VOCALS

The Chain.

From the first sound to the recorded performance, every stage has a place in the Haerviu system.

AUDIO SYSTEM

Hardware → Mix → DAW

The MC-707 and TR-8S feed the studio mix while the MX-1 acts as the central audio hub. The finished signal reaches Cubase for arrangement, editing and final production.

MC-707 → TR-8S → MX-1 → CUBASE
VOCAL SYSTEM

Voice → Process → Mix

The RØDE NT1 captures the voice, the VT-4 becomes the creative processing stage, and the processed signal returns to the studio for recording and production.

NT1 → VT-4 → MX-1 → CUBASE
